Who can play

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You must be legally allowed to take part in games like these where you live.
  • You may hold one account. Accounts must be in your own name, and you must give accurate information.
  • We may ask you to verify your identity or bank account before payouts.

How the games work

Hourly Cash. 24 rounds run each day. A round opens on the hour and closes 50 minutes later. You pick a number from 1 to 5 before it closes. When the round settles, players' picks are combined into the crowd's value for that hour, and gains depend on how close your pick is to it. Once submitted, a pick cannot be changed.

Hide or Guess. One player hides a number from 1 to 5 behind a card and sets a stake. Another player pays that stake to guess the number. A right guess wins; a wrong guess loses the stake.

Auto Play. If you enable Auto Play, the app submits your chosen figure for the rounds you select just before they close. The app must be open for it to run, and you are responsible for the figures and rounds you set.

The demo games on our website use pretend money and have no real payouts. They are only a preview.

Wallet, deposits and payouts

  • You fund your wallet by deposit and can request a payout to your linked, verified bank account.
  • Stakes and results are applied to your wallet balance as rounds settle.
  • We may delay or refuse a payout where we need to verify your identity or account, suspect fraud, or must comply with the law.
  • Bank or payment-provider fees, where they apply, will be shown before you confirm.

Risk

Games of chance and skill involve risk. You can lose the stake you enter. Only play with money you can afford to lose, and never borrow to play. See Responsible Use for tools and tips.

What you must not do

  • Use bots, scripts or other tools to gain an unfair edge, other than the Auto Play feature we provide.

  • Use CrowdGames for money laundering or any illegal purpose.
