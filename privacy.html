Who we are

CrowdGames ("we", "us") provides a mobile app with two games: Hourly Cash and Hide or Guess. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the app and to this website.

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other laws that apply to us.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to run the games and your wallet:

  • Account details: your name, phone number, and a password or sign-in credential.
  • Wallet and payment details: deposits, game entries, results, payout requests, and the bank account you link for withdrawals.
  • Device information: a device identifier, device type, and notification token, used to keep your account secure and send alerts.
  • Contacts (only if you allow it): when you choose to invite friends, the app reads your contacts so you can pick who to invite. You decide who receives an invite.
  • Gameplay data: your picks, stakes, results, Auto Play settings and round history.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you ask for help.

Biometric lock

If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, the check happens on your phone using your device's own system. We do not receive, see or store your fingerprint or face data.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and run your wallet.
  • To run rounds, settle results and pay out winnings.
  • To verify bank accounts and process deposits and withdrawals.
  • To send alerts such as rounds opening, results and account news.
  • To prevent fraud, underage use and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.
  • To improve the app and answer support requests.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Payment and banking partners that process deposits, verify bank accounts and send payouts.
  • Service providers that host our systems, deliver notifications and help us keep the service running, under contracts that limit how they use your data.
  • Authorities when the law requires it or when needed to protect users and prevent fraud.

Storage and security

Sensitive information on your device is stored in encrypted storage. Data on our systems is protected with access controls and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password and phone safe and tell us right away if you think your account was accessed without your permission.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long afterwards as needed to meet legal, accounting and fraud-prevention requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict certain uses, and withdraw consent you previously gave. You can also turn off notifications and contact access in your phone settings at any time. To make a request, email us at the address below. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Age limit

CrowdGames is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to create an account. If we learn that someone under 18 has registered, we will close the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell you in the app. Continuing to use CrowdGames after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions?

Email us at support@crowdstock.app and we'll get back to you.
