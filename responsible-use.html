Our promise

CrowdGames is meant to be entertainment. It is never a way to make a living, solve money problems or recover losses. We want playing to stay fun and within your means.

18+ only

You must be 18 or older to play. If you share a phone or tablet with someone younger, please keep your account and wallet locked. Turn on biometric lock in the app so nobody else can open it.

Simple rules to play by

  • Only play with money you can afford to lose.
  • Decide how much you'll spend before you start and stick to it.
  • Never borrow money, or use rent, school fees or bills money, to play.
  • Don't chase losses. A losing run doesn't make the next win more likely.
  • Take breaks. Step away if you feel stressed, angry or low.
  • Don't play when you're tired or have been drinking.

Signs it may be becoming a problem

  • Spending more time or money than you planned.
  • Thinking about the next round all the time.
  • Hiding your play from family or friends.
  • Borrowing or selling things to play.
  • Feeling anxious or guilty about playing, or playing to escape other problems.

Staying in control

You can switch off notifications, turn off Auto Play, and stop depositing at any time. If you want a break from CrowdGames, or want your account closed, email us and we'll help.

Where to get help

If you or someone close to you is struggling, please talk to someone you trust, or contact a local counselling or mental health service. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Questions?

Email us at support@crowdstock.app and we'll get back to you.
