FAQ

Quick answers.

What is CrowdGames?

A mobile app with two games: Hourly Cash, where you pick a number from 1 to 5 each hour, and Hide or Guess, where you hide a number or guess another player's for an instant result.

How many Hourly Cash rounds are there a day?

24. One opens every hour on the hour and stays open for 50 minutes.

Can I change my pick after I submit it?

No. Once submitted, your pick is locked until the round settles.

Can I lose money?

In Hide or Guess, a wrong guess costs your entry stake. In Hourly Cash, your gain depends on how close your pick lands to the crowd's value. Only play with money you can afford to spend.

What is Auto Play?

Set a figure for the rounds you choose and the app submits for you just before each one closes. Keep the app open so it can fire.

How do withdrawals work?

Link a verified bank account, then request a payout from your wallet.

Is my account secure?

Yes. The app supports biometric lock and stores sensitive data encrypted on your device.
